SECURITY

Your drawings. Your prices. Nobody else's.

Each company on Forge runs in its own isolated space. Your customers' drawings and your pricing are never visible to anyone else on the platform.

Tenant isolation at the database

Data is separated per company at the database level, not just hidden in the interface.

Role-based access

You decide who can edit and who can only view. New staff start with read-only access.

Multi-factor sign-in

Accounts can be protected with a second factor.

Customer and supplier portals

Customers and suppliers get their own sign-in, with access kept separate from your staff's.
